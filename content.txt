Q: What is an Invoice?
An invoice is a document sent by a seller to a buyer requesting payment for goods or services provided. It includes details like item descriptions, quantities, prices, taxes, and payment terms.

Q: Purpose of an Invoice
To request payment from a customer. To maintain a record of sales and transactions. To track receivables and manage cash flow. To ensure compliance with tax laws (e.g., GST, VAT).

Q: Difference Between Invoice and Bill
Invoice : Sent by the seller to the buyer requesting payment. Bill : Received by the buyer from the seller and represents an amount owed.

Q: Common Terms Used in Invoicing
Due Date : The deadline for payment. PO Number : Purchase Order number, used for tracking orders. Line Items : Individual products or services listed on the invoice. Tax : Applicable taxes like GST or VAT. Subtotal : Total cost before taxes or discounts. Balance Due : Amount remaining to be paid.

Q: Types of Invoices
Proforma Invoice : A preliminary invoice sent before delivery. Recurring Invoice : Automatically generated for subscriptions or repeat services. Credit Note : Issued to adjust overcharged amounts or provide refunds.
