


#How to Use Zoho Books Invoices
 Creating an Invoice
1. Go to **Sales → Invoices**.
2. Click **+ New** in the top right corner.
3. Fill in details like customer name, items, taxes, and payment terms.
4. Save and send the invoice to your customer.

# Editing an Invoice
1. Go to **Sales → Invoices**.
2. Select the invoice you want to edit.
3. Click **Edit** and make the necessary changes.
4. Save the updated invoice.

# Sending an Invoice
1. Go to **Sales → Invoices**.
2. Select the invoice you want to send.
3. Click **Mail / SMS** and choose the method (email, SMS, or snail mail).
4. Send the invoice to your customer.

# Recording Payments
 Online Payments
1. Integrate Zoho Books with a payment gateway (e.g., PayPal, Razorpay).
2. When creating an invoice, select the preferred payment gateway.
3. Customers can pay via email, invoice link, or Customer Portal.

# Charging a Customer’s Card
1. Go to **Sales → Invoices**.
2. Select the invoice and click **Record Payment > Charge Customer**.
3. Enter the amount and payment gateway details.
4. Process the payment.

# Recording Offline Payments
1. Go to **Sales → Invoices**.
2. Select the invoice and click **Record Payment > Record Offline Payment**.
3. Enter payment details (amount received, date, deposit account).
4. Save the payment.

# Bill of Supply
#What is a Bill of Supply?
A Bill of Supply is issued by GST-registered businesses for exempted goods or under the Composition Scheme.

#How to Create a Bill of Supply
1. Go to **Settings > Preferences > General** and enable the Bill of Supply option.
2. Go to **Sales > Invoices > New Bill of Supply**.
3. Add customer details and other necessary information.
4. Save and send the Bill of Supply.

# Payments Received
1. View payments received under **Sales > Payments Received**.
2. Edit or delete payments if needed.
3. Add custom fields for additional details.


# Exporting and Bulk Actions
1. **Export Invoices**: Export invoices in CSV, XLS, or XLSX formats.
2. **Bulk Update**: Update multiple invoices at once.
3. **Download as ZIP**: Download multiple invoices as individual PDFs in a ZIP file.

# Additional Features
1. **Discounts**: Apply discounts at the item level or invoice level.
2. **Shipping Charges and Adjustments**: Add these below the item table.
3. **Clone Invoices**: Duplicate existing invoices for similar transactions.
4. **Attach Files**: Attach up to 5 files (5MB each) to invoices.
5. **Void or Write Off Invoices**: Void invoices to remove them from reports or write off unpaid invoices.
